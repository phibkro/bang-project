## Reuse protocol

1. State the active mission pressure.
2. Link the exact historical declaration, test, or decision being considered.
3. Re-evaluate it under the current constitution.
4. Record whether it is adopted, adapted, or rejected and why.
5. Copy code only with license and provenance intact.

Do not merge the repositories wholesale. Their architectures encode different product theses and unresolved assumptions.
